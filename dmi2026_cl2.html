§0.3 Objectives

This course is designed for computer engineering undergraduate students. Its aim is to introduce the terminology, available methods, and ideas in discrete mathematics to future computer scientists and engineers.

§2 Class policy

  • Any devices or other equipment that distracts you, other students, or the instructor should not be used during lectures except in emergencies.
  • Missing a lecture can — and probably will — lead to a domino effect. You're responsible for everything covered in class, so if you miss a session:
    • Photocopy and read notes from someone who was there.
    • Read the relevant sections in the lecture notes and textbooks, and search online if needed.
    Once you've done that, feel free to contact the instructor if anything still needs clarifying.

§3 Academic honesty

☞ Not knowing the academic honesty policy is not a reasonable excuse for violating it.

Academic fraud is punished severely. Depending on the nature and severity of the offense — as judged by the instructor, not the offender — possible sanctions include but aren't limited to:

  1. A grade of zero on the assignment.
  2. A final grade of zero for the whole course.
  3. Referral to the disciplinary committee.
