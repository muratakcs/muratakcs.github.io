Course objective

This course is designed for computer engineering undergraduate students. Its aim is to introduce the terminology, available methods, and ideas in discrete mathematics to future computer scientists and engineers.

Class policy, rules, and regulations

  • Devices or other equipment that distract you, other students, or the instructor should not be used during lectures except in emergencies.
  • Missing a lecture can—and probably will—lead to a domino effect. If you miss a lecture, you are strongly encouraged to study the material you missed before returning to class. You are responsible for all material covered in class. In case you miss a lecture:
    • Photocopy and read notes from someone who was in class.
    • Read the relevant sections from the lecture notes and textbooks, and search the Internet if necessary.
    After completing these steps, you may contact the instructor if you need clarification on any material.

Academic honesty

Lack of knowledge of the academic honesty policy is not a reasonable explanation for a violation.

Any academic fraud will be severely punished. Depending on the nature and severity of the offense—as evaluated by the instructor rather than the offender—possible sanctions include, but are not limited to: (1) assigning a grade of zero to the assignment; (2) assigning a final grade of zero for the whole course; and (3) referral to the disciplinary committee.
