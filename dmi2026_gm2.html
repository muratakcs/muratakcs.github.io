Objectives

This course is designed for computer engineering undergraduate students. Its aim is to introduce the terminology, available methods, and ideas in discrete mathematics to future computer scientists/engineers.

Class Policy, Rules, and Regulations

  • Any devices or other equipment that distracts you, other students, or the instructor should not be used during lectures except in emergencies.
  • Missing a lecture can (and probably will) lead to a domino effect. If you miss a lecture, you are strongly recommended to study the material you missed before you return to class. You are responsible for all the material covered in class. So, in case you miss a lecture:
    • Photocopy, and read notes from someone who was in class.
    • Read the relevant sections from the lecture notes, textbooks, and make an Internet search if necessary.
  • After you have done this, you may contact the instructor if you need clarification on any materials.
